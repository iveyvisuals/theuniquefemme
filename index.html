FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the conference?

Embodied takes place over two days at the Watson Porter Pavilion. May 1st is The UNIQUEfemme Conference and May 2nd is the Integration Experience.

What's the difference between the two days?

Day One is our full day conference with speakers, breakout mastermind sessions, embodiment activations and immersive activities. Day Two is the Integration day, a slower space to sink deeper into the learnings, sit in community and book sessions with facilitators.

What kind of sessions happen on Integration day?

You can expect sessions and experiences in breathwork, journaling, human design, an ecstatic dance party, yoga and more, plus bookable sessions in reiki, fitness coaching, human design and business strategy.
